# OUL 

Universal Cheat Script For Roblox

Combat ---
- Aimbot -- Prediction, Pull Strength, Fov Circle
- Ragebot -- Above, Below, Behind - (Constantly Teleports Behind Nearest Or Selected Player)
- TriggerBot -- Delay (ms)
- SilentAim - (Experimental) - (Directs All Bullets Towards The Targets Head)
- AntiKatana - (Soon)(Rivals*)
- VoidSpam -- Above, Below, Behind, AutoAttack, VRoam, VoidInterval, AttackInterval - (Teleports The Player A Set Distance Out Into The Void And Teleports Back To The Player After A Period Of Time)
  
Movement ---
- Vortex - (Buggy/ Experimental - (Splits The Characters Parts Into An Orbit Around The Center Of Your Hitbox))
- Fly - (Up To 1000 Studs Per Second)
- Noclip - (Allows Player To Phase Through Walls)
- Infinite Jump - (This Is Self Explanatory)
- Speed - (Up To 250 Studs Per Second)
  
Visuals ---
- Radar - (Shows Players On A Minimap)
- ESP -- Skeleton, Box, Tracers, Chams, Health, Usernames
- FullBright - (Removes All Shadows)
- NoFog - (Removes All Haze/ Fog In The Game)
- SkyBox - (Allows User To Set A Custom Sky Colour)

Theme---
- Gradient - (Adds A Gradient Colour Behind The Menu)
- MenuSnow - (Adds Snow Particles Behind The Menu For Style)
- Styles -- Basic, Modern, Classic OUL - (Changes The Menu Into Different Styles, Basic: Blocky Modern: More Rounded Classic OUL: A Mix Of The Both)
  
Menu ---
- Configs -- AutoLoad (Lets The User Save Settings And Then Load Them All At Once When Load Button Is Pressed)
- Keybind Editor - (Lets The User Change Keybinds That Toggle Or Activate Selected Actions)
- ShowKeybinds - (Shows A List Of The Keybinds Attached To Actions)
- ShowWaterMark - (Shows The OUL Logo And The Clients FPS And Ping)
